Ground rules for using CycleCare responsibly

Version 2026-07-19. CycleCare is an educational portfolio application and is not a medical diagnostic service.

Explicit consent required

Use Of The Service

CycleCare helps users record and understand menstrual-cycle and wellness patterns. It should not replace professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Your Account

You are responsible for keeping login credentials secure and for entering accurate information. CycleCare stores passwords as BCrypt hashes and never displays another user's records.

Health Information

You control whether to enter cycle, symptom, mood, wellness, and journal data. These records are used to provide dashboards, analytics, reports, reminders, and personalized insights.

Policy Updates

If the Privacy Policy or Terms version changes, you must review and accept the updated version before continuing to protected areas of the application.

Account Deletion

You may delete your account from the profile page after password confirmation. Deletion is irreversible and removes account-linked personal health data.

Limitations

This implementation demonstrates production-grade privacy engineering concepts for a portfolio project. It does not claim legal compliance or create a clinician-patient relationship.
