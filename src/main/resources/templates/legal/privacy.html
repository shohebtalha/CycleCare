Privacy Policy

How CycleCare handles sensitive health data

Version 2026-07-19. This portfolio implementation is inspired by responsible data-protection practices and DPDP Act principles, without representing formal legal compliance.

No sale of personal data

Data Collected

CycleCare collects account details such as name, email, and password hash, plus optional profile information such as age, height, weight, and activity level.

Health records may include cycle dates, flow entries, symptoms, moods, sleep, water intake, journal notes, nutrition logs, reports, and health-intelligence outputs generated from your own records.

Purpose

Data is collected to provide cycle tracking, wellness summaries, calendar views, report generation, reminders, and personalized health insights inside CycleCare.

CycleCare requests only data needed for these product experiences and keeps consent records with the active policy version and timestamp.

Use And Sharing

CycleCare uses your data to operate your account, personalize dashboards, produce analytics, and support account security workflows like password resets.

CycleCare does not sell personal data. Data is not exposed to other users, and user-owned records are queried through the authenticated account context.

Protection

Passwords remain hashed with BCrypt. Access-controlled endpoints validate the authenticated user before reading, modifying, exporting, or deleting personal health records.

Application secrets are designed to be supplied through environment variables in production configuration.

Your Choices

You can update profile details, export reports, and delete your account from the profile page. Deletion requires password confirmation and removes account-linked health records.

When the Privacy Policy or Terms version changes, CycleCare will require acceptance of the updated version before continuing into authenticated app features.

Retention

CycleCare keeps account and health records while your account exists. Consent records are retained with your account to show which policy version was accepted.

After account deletion, account-linked records are deleted through database relationships and service-layer cleanup.
