Consent & Data Usage

What you consent to when using CycleCare

Current version 2026-07-19. Consent is explicit, versioned, and recorded with a timestamp.

Purpose-bound data use
1

Collection

You provide account information and optional health records such as cycle dates, symptoms, moods, sleep, hydration, flow, and journal notes.

2

Use

CycleCare uses this data for dashboards, history views, reminders, reports, analytics, and AI-assisted health-intelligence features.

3

Protection

Records are scoped to your authenticated account. Passwords use BCrypt, and sensitive configuration belongs in environment variables.

4

Control

You can update profile data, export reports, or delete your account. CycleCare does not sell personal data.
